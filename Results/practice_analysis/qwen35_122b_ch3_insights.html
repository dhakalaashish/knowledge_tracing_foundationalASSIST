Practice insights from the Qwen 122B Chapter 3 labels

Each FoundationalASSIST problem is labeled by Qwen3.5-122B (thinking) with the standard prompts plus the full text of NAEP Chapter 3 (run qwen35_122b_ch3): the practices a student needs, in order, and the top practice, the step where students most likely go wrong. Questions 1–21 use the top practice, as in the earlier page made from the Qwen 30B v3 labels (question 15, on common wrong answers by top practice, was replaced by Q50, which uses the diagnoses; the other numbers are kept); questions 22–29 use all the practices a problem needs; 30–31 and Part H (32–38) cover skills: how many practices each skill has, and which; Part I (39–42) asks whether work on a prerequisite or dependent skill (MACA-CD skill graph) carries over, within the same practice; Part J (43–51) analyzes the model's diagnoses of students' common wrong answers (step 6): which practice step fails, and what kind of error. Parts K–M (52–65) label every response with what its answer shows (correct; wrong after help; a diagnosed shared wrong answer with its failed step; a rare or one-off answer) and ask whether the top practice names the step that actually failed (K), how often students slip or guess (L), and whether a student's errors are consistent over time (M). Highlighted boxes explain how a question was answered and what its result does and does not show. The responses are the cleaned MACA-CD data: each student's first attempt at each problem, scored 0 or 1, students and problems with at least 30 responses.

Units. Differences in correctness are in percentage points. "Adjusted" results compare each answer with what is expected for that problem (its leave-one-out % correct) and that student (their average over all problems), so neither harder problems nor stronger students explain them. Collaborative Mathematics is the top practice of 23 answered problems (0.8% of responses), so its numbers are noisy and it is left out of the learning questions (16–21). All 3,395 problems have Chapter 3 labels: 16 that failed at first were labeled in a retry (14 of them with the order filled in, because their answer 1 lists a single practice).
